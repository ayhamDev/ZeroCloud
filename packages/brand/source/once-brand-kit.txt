Here are the **non-looped (play-once)** versions of the **Micro-Dot Cut** animations. 

Each icon sweeps into place diagonally from upper-left to lower-right in under `1 second` with a crisp micro-snap, then locks cleanly into its final static state (`animation-fill-mode: both` prevents any flicker before the delay starts).

### Pro Tip for Web/App Developers
* **Replay on Transfer Complete or Hover:** Since these play once on mount, in React you can replay the animation anytime (e.g., when a P2P transfer finishes) by incrementing a `key` prop on the `<svg key={replayCount}>`, or in vanilla JS by cloning/re-inserting the SVG node.
