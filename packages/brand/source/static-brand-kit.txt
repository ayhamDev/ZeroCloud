Here is the complete **ZeroCloud Brand Kit** built around **#2 (Micro-Dot Cut Variant)**—covering **No Words** and **With Words**, **all sizes**, **all monochrome/background variations**, and **self-contained animated SVGs**.

## 1. Sizes Guide & Tiny-Size (`16px–24px`) Icons

Because SVGs use `viewBox`, every logo below scales cleanly to any size (`32`, `48`, `64`, `128`, `256`, `512`) just by changing `width` and `height`. 

However, for **16px–24px** (browser favicons, status bars), use these micro-tuned versions with slightly thicker dots (`r="2.85"` and `r="1.7"`) so the cut stays visible on low-DPI screens:
